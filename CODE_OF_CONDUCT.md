# Code of conduct

Character Planner follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and
commit messages) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

In short: be welcoming, respectful and constructive, and assume good faith. Harassment,
insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, and publishing
someone's private information without their permission aren't acceptable, and nor is
anything else a reasonable person would see as inappropriate in a professional setting.
The full text linked above is what counts.

## Reporting

Email **contact@durishlabs.com** to report behaviour that breaks this code. Reports are
read by the maintainer, kept private, and answered within a week. You don't need proof,
and you won't be identified to the person you report without your permission.

If the report is about the maintainer, say so in the email. It will be handled with the
same privacy, and you can also report to GitHub through its
[content reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

For a security vulnerability, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and decides what to do, using the Contributor
Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
Depending on what happened, the response ranges from a private correction to a warning,
a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project's spaces. Whoever reported it hears
the outcome, unless that would put someone else's privacy at risk.
